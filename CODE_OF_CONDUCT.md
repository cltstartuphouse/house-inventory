# Code of conduct

CLT Startup House is a place where people learn by building next to each other. This repo works the same way.

## What we expect

- Be generous with what you know, and patient with people who are newer than you.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something's unclear.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't post anyone's personal information, photos or code without their permission.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults or demeaning comments, in issues, pull requests, commit messages or in person.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Deliberately adding harmful, deceptive or malicious code.
- Publishing someone else's private information.

## What happens

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close pull requests and block anyone who doesn't follow this code, here or at the house.

## Reporting

Tell a host at the house in person, or reach out through [cltstartuphouse.com](https://cltstartuphouse.com). We'll keep it private and follow up.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
